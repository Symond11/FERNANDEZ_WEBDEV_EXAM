AI is taking over the world, protect your privacy.

Welcome to Haven, a lightweight social media platform built for our web development project. People can connect, share ideas, and express themselves freely without worrying about corporate AI systems spying on them or collecting their personal data.

Create an Account Log In View Feed

Platform Features:

1. Zero AI Scraping

Posts are marked to prevent automated AI crawler bots from harvesting your words into training datasets.

2. Client-Side Security

All accounts, credentials, and posts are saved safely inside your browser's local sandbox.

3. Metadata Stripper

Removes device trackers, cookies, and location tags so your personal information is never exposed.
