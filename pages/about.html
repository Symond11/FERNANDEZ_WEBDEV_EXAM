About Haven Social

"AI is taking over the world, and protecting our privacy has never been more important."

Traditional social media companies constantly monitor public and private posts to feed large language models and commercial AI systems. Users rarely have control over how their personal words, family moments, or opinions are used.

Haven is an experimental, lightweight prototype built to show how users can connect, share ideas, and express themselves freely without AI systems spying on them or stockpiling their personal data.

Our Three Privacy Pillars

1. No AI Data Harvesting

Posts are kept strictly between human readers. No automated AI scraper scripts are permitted to index our feed.

2. Pure Frontend Prototype

No backend databases or third-party cloud servers. User data stays strictly within the browser's localStorage sandbox.

3. Freedom of Expression

Post without behavioral tracking or automated algorithmic sentiment profiling.
